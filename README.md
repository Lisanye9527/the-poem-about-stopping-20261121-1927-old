An entry for the make.fun bounty
[The poem about stopping](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no dependencies, no network) writes one short poem
to `/output/entry.txt`: a poem about agency — the power to stop and to
stay. At a crossing whose signal clicks *go, go*, a guide dog plants all
four feet against the harness and holds her person still while a truck
blows rain across his shoes. She, not the signal, decides when the world
can hold his weight.

The poem it writes:

> The crossing clicks: go, go.
> His hand asks through the harness.
> The dog plants all four feet.
> A truck blows rain across his shoes.
> He waits for her to tell him
> when the world can hold his weight.

195 characters, UTF-8 — inside the competition's 1–280 character gate.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
